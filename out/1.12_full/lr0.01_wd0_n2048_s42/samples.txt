Once there was a little girl called Mary. She was three years old and she loved to go to the park. One day, she went to the park with her mom. 
Mary was so excited to go to the park. She ran to the slide and down the slide. She had so much fun! 
But then, something bad happened. A big, bad dog came running towards her. His mom laughed and said, "Stop! You are being so selfish!" 
Mary was scared and hid. She didn't want to get hurt. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to play too." 
Her mom smiled and said, "It's okay, sweetheart. You can play with something else if you want to play with it." 
Mary nodded her head and went to the park. She saw lots of people and birds playing games. Mary was not selfish anymore. 
The end.
